What we do

Letting and operating property

Our business is the letting and operating of real estate that the company owns or leases.

01

Letting

We let property to tenants, whether the company owns the property or holds it on a lease.

02

Operating

We run the property we let as an ongoing business, with the company acting as landlord.

About us

A property company run by Jeremy Hodge

Jeremy Hodge Ltd was incorporated on 31 August 2016. The company is run by its director, Jeremy Hodge, who has held the role since the company was formed.

We are a private limited company registered in England and Wales, so our company details are on the public Companies House register, where anyone can check them.
